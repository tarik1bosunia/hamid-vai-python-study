# Gene Structure & Transcript Overview

A clean, well-structured explanation of the major components involved in gene expression.

---

## **1. mRNA (cDNA) Sequence**

* **mRNA**: RNA copy made from DNA.
* **cDNA**: DNA version of mRNA created in labs.
* Represents the transcript that may be translated into protein.

---

## **2. Main Regions of mRNA**

### **5′UTR (5' Untranslated Region)**

* Before the **start codon (AUG)**.
* Does **not** code for protein.
* Important for translation regulation.

### **CDS (Coding Sequence)**

* Between **start codon (AUG)** and **stop codon (UAA/UAG/UGA)**.
* Encodes the **amino acid sequence**.

### **3′UTR (3' Untranslated Region)**

* After the **stop codon**.
* Does **not** code for protein.
* Controls mRNA stability, lifespan, and translation efficiency.

---

## **3. Amino Acid Sequence**

* Produced from the **CDS** during translation.
* Each **codon (3 bases)** → **1 amino acid**.
* Only for **protein-coding transcripts**.

---

## **4. Promoter Region**

* Located **upstream (before)** the transcription start site.
* Contains regulatory elements like **TATA box**.
* RNA polymerase binds here to **initiate transcription**.

---

## **5. Terminator Region**

* Located **downstream (after)** the transcription end site.
* Signals RNA polymerase to **stop transcription**.
* Ensures proper release of mRNA.

---

## **6. Simple Visual Overview**

```
DNA:     Promoter ---- Gene (CDS) ---- Terminator
                 ↓ Transcription
mRNA:    5'UTR ---- CDS ---- 3'UTR
                 ↓ Translation
Protein: Amino Acid Sequence
```
